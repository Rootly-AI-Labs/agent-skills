# On-call outputs

## Schedule or personal view

Identify the person, covered schedules/services, exact shift bounds and time
zone, current responder, next handoff, and any overlapping responsibilities.
When requested, include incidents the person is responding to and their open
follow-ups, with scope limits. Do not infer ownership from service membership.

## Shift handoff

- Outgoing/incoming people, shift window/time zone, and source coverage.
- Alerts and incidents touched, each outcome, and actionable/noisy distinctions.
- Loose ends, follow-ups, active incidents needing an explicit transfer.
- Upcoming events, deploys, and maintenance windows, or unavailable data.
- Pending ownership acknowledgments and the next useful checks.

## Coverage proposal

- Operational reason, interval, service scope, and expertise/access required.
- Current duties and contextualized load, including relevant overnight paging.
- Candidate, source of explicit availability, all-schedule/policy overlap checks,
  calendar/PTO coverage, and missing checks.
- Whose agreement is required. Say that no override or swap was applied.

Keep personal details minimal. A score is not a health diagnosis; a free-looking
calendar is not proof of service access or willingness to cover.
