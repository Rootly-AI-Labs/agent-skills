# Review packet

- Incident identity, recorded severity/status, dates, review scope, and proposed
  owner/scheduling follow-up if no owner is recorded.
- Sourced timeline, impact window/charts, response actions, and missing evidence.
- Depth selected using company policy: concise async packet or deeper response,
  alerting, and interview preparation.
- Confirmed contributing factors separated from competing explanations, with
  evidence for/against and proposed ways to settle important disputes.
- Facilitation questions about technical and organizational conditions.
- Learning/conclusions supplied by humans, attributed, or explicit questions
  for the review participants. No agent-authored final conclusions.
- Action proposals: evidence, intended improvement, verification method, roadmap
  owner to review, and decision state (proposed, accepted/scheduled, or declined
  with reason). Preserve actual existing decisions; invent neither dates nor owners.

Use only audience-authorized evidence to select specific questions and proposals.
Excluded interview material must not shape questions, hints, or anonymous summaries;
do not ask participants to elaborate on its context. Generic facilitation prompts
must stand independently of restricted material. If needed, say only that
restricted material was excluded; request approved synthesis without naming its topic.
